AI features fail when they are treated as magic instead of systems.

## Design the workflow first

Trigger → AI → Processing → Database → Notification → Action. If any step is unclear, the product will be unclear.

## Prefer orchestration over prompts alone

n8n and custom API layers let you retry, log, and branch. Prompts are only one unit in the pipeline.

## Measure business outcomes

Track time saved, tickets resolved, or conversion lift — not just model latency.

That is how AI becomes infrastructure instead of a slideshow.
